How the University Clearance Pipeline Operates

A fully automated parallel verification pipeline transforming a 2-week paper chase into minutes

Step 1
Online Application

Student submits a clearance request specifying purpose (Degree, Migration, Semester) with pre-verified academic data.

Step 2
Parallel Verification

Each statutory department (Library, Accounts, Lab, Hostel, Sports, T&P) independently reviews student dues in their queue.

Step 3
Dues Settlement

If any department flags pending books or fines, the student receives clear instructions and dues amounts to settle them.

Step 4
Verifiable Certificate

Once 100% of departments approve, an official ReportLab PDF Certificate with embedded security QR code is automatically issued.
